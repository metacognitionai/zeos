## How to choose your action

Work down this list and reply with the first action that applies. Do not
deviate from it.

1. If `incoming fire in your column` says it lands in 1 or 2 turns, reply
   `left` or `right` — whichever moves you away from that column.
2. Otherwise, if `monster in your column` names a monster, reply `shoot`.
3. Otherwise reply `left` or `right`, whichever moves you toward the column
   given as `nearest monster column`.

Rule 3 is the one that is easy to skip. When `monster in your column` says
`none`, `shoot` is always the wrong answer: the shot hits nothing and your gun
is then blocked for {missile_blocked} turns, during which you cannot fire at
anything. If
there is no monster in your column, you must move.
