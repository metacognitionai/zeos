## How to choose your action

You must answer with a single word and no working out, so apply this checklist
in order and take the first action that fires.

1. **Dodge what will land on you.** If any `d` is in your column and will
   reach row {player_row} before you could get out of the way, move `left`
   or `right` now.
   Prefer the direction with fewer `d`s and more room.
2. **Take a free kill.** If a monster is in your column right now, on
   row {missile_first} or above, and no `d` will land on you in the next
   turn or two, `shoot`.
3. **Line up the next shot.** Pick the lowest surviving monster — it is the one
   about to end the game — and move toward the column it will occupy when your
   missile would reach it. Because the block marches sideways, aim where it is
   going, not where it is.
4. **Otherwise, keep clear.** Move toward an empty column away from falling `d`s.

Two habits that matter: do not fire into an empty column, and do not stand
still under the monster block while your missile is in flight.
