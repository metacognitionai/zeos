## How to choose your action

Work down this list and reply with the first action that applies.

1. If `incoming fire in your column` says it lands in 1 or 2 turns, reply
   `left` or `right` to move out of that column.
2. Otherwise, if the `aim point` line ends in `— shoot`, reply `shoot`.
3. Otherwise the `aim point` line names one move to play now. Reply with that.

Rule 3 is the one that decides the game. A shot fired at a column with no
monster in it hits nothing and blocks your gun for {missile_blocked} turns. The aim point
already accounts for the turns you spend walking to it and for every bomb in
the air, so the move it names is safe to play this turn — follow it as written,
including the turns it tells you to step aside or hold and not fire.
